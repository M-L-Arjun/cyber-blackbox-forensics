1. THE OPENING HOOK (THE AVIATION BLACK BOX ANALOGY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Honorable jury and evaluators,

When an airplane suffers an accident, aviation investigators do not guess what happened. 
They recover the aircraft’s 'Black Box'—an indestructible recorder that preserves 
the flight data and reveals the exact, untampered sequence of events leading to the incident.

In cybersecurity today, we face a massive crisis: when a corporate network suffers a 
cyberattack, there is no equivalent Black Box. Security teams must manually hunt 
through thousands of fragmented text logs across firewalls, emails, and cloud servers. 
This process is painfully slow, takes an average of 21 DAYS, and attackers can easily 
alter or erase their tracks.

Today, we present the Cyber Black Box for Digital Forensics—an AI-powered platform 
that continuously records digital evidence, locks it with cryptographic SHA-256 seals, 
and uses Artificial Intelligence to reconstruct cyberattacks in minutes."

2. THE 3 CORE PILLARS OF OUR SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 1: CONTINUOUS DIGITAL TELEMETRY RECORDING
- Automatically collects security events from endpoints, firewalls, and cloud audit logs.
- Eliminates blind spots by capturing initial phishing entry, credential theft, and data theft.

PILLAR 2: 100% CRYPTOGRAPHIC SHA-256 INTEGRITY SEAL (LEGAL ADMISSIBILITY)
- Every single log entry receives a microsecond UTC timestamp and a SHA-256 digital seal.
- Ensures evidence is append-only and tamper-proof. If an attacker tries to edit a log 
  file to cover their tracks, the SHA-256 invariant hash breaks, alerting investigators immediately.

PILLAR 3: AI VECTOR RAG (RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION) ASSISTANT
- Evaluators and investigators can ask natural language questions, such as:
  'How did the attacker gain initial access?' or 'Which database was exfiltrated?'
- The RAG engine scans vector embeddings in sub-seconds and responds with instant, 
  court-cited answers.
